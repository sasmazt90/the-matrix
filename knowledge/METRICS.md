# Metric dictionary for the synthetic example
Plan and actual spend use EUR for the same market, campaign and period. Line gap = actual minus plan. Campaign pacing variance = total gap / total plan.
Finance supplies a single campaign-level contribution before media. After-media contribution subtracts total media spend exactly once. Missing finance is unknown. Costs in the local example are whole euros.
A measured ads-on versus ads-off campaign contrast does not identify the effect of a 10% cut to a single line item. To decide the cut, define a comparison against current spend, confirm finance coverage, agree the randomisation unit, outcome, horizon and capacity constraints. The sample contains no arm estimates or confidence intervals.
